# Activity 1

## Question
How will you solve the problem of copying, without any support from management?

## Answer

To reduce copying without management support, I will focus on creating awareness and using smart methods during tests and assignments.

1. I will prepare different question sets for students.
2. I will arrange seating properly to reduce communication.
3. I will encourage students to understand concepts instead of memorizing answers.
4. I will conduct surprise oral questions and small activities.
5. I will make assignments more practical and creative so students write their own answers.
6. I will monitor students carefully during exams.
7. I will motivate students by explaining the importance of honesty and self-learning.

These steps can help reduce copying and improve genuine learning even without management support.
